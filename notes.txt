Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines.
It enables computers to learn from data, recognize patterns, and make decisions.

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that focuses on training models using data.
Deep Learning is a type of ML that uses neural networks with multiple layers.

AI is used in many real-world applications such as:
- Healthcare (disease detection, medical imaging)
- Finance (fraud detection, trading)
- Education (personalized learning)
- Automation (self-driving cars, robotics)

Advantages of AI:
- Reduces human effort
- Improves efficiency
- Handles large data easily

Challenges of AI:
- Requires large data
- Can be biased
- Expensive to build and maintain
